How it works

From surplus to support
in four steps

  1. 01

    Offer or request

    Donors post what they can give; beneficiaries describe what they need.

  2. 02

    Verify & match

    Admins verify NGO documents. NGOs review requests and match by proximity.

  3. 03

    Pick up & deliver

    NGOs accept, schedule the pickup and deliver aid to verified beneficiaries.

  4. 04

    Track the impact

    Every status change is logged, so everyone can see where help went.

Transparency & trust

  • Verified NGOs & beneficiariesDocuments are reviewed by admins before anyone can receive aid.
  • Real-time status trackingDonated → Accepted → Picked up → Delivered, visible to donor and NGO.
  • Audits & impact reportsAdmin activity logs and reports keep every organisation accountable.
